# 07. Science and message

Status: draft 0.3, 2026-11-13. Items marked **to check** are from memory and need their reference confirmed before release.

## How the game carries its message

- Play says nothing. The player feels hungry, afraid, relieved and accompanied as a shrimp. That is the message (D3).
- Facts live in the journal, one or two sentences each, unlocked by doing the thing they describe.
- There is no closing card and no call to action in the game.
- The About page lists the sources and the credits. The game is a personal project and names no organisation (D5).

## Rules for every journal text

1. It describes shrimp. It never addresses the player and never says what anyone should do.
2. No moral words: cruel, worst, suffering, victim, innocent.
3. It says what was observed and who observed it. It does not argue.
4. Two sentences at most. A third only for a source.
5. It never says shrimp eat only plants. It is silent on diet beyond what the game shows (D7, D8).
6. It names the animal a study used when that animal is not a whiteleg shrimp.
7. Every card carries a source from the table below.

## Journal cards: "What shrimp feel"

Draft texts in both languages. Each unlocks from the event listed.

**1. Scent / L'odorat.** Unlocks: first use of Sense. Source: to check.
- EN: A shrimp's antennae are covered in sensors that taste the water. It can follow a trail to food, or to another shrimp, in the dark.
- FR: Les antennes d'une crevette sont couvertes de capteurs qui goûtent l'eau. Elle peut suivre une piste jusqu'à sa nourriture, ou jusqu'à une autre crevette, dans le noir.

**2. The tail flip / Le coup de queue.** Unlocks: third tail flip. Source: to check.
- EN: One snap of the tail throws a shrimp backward faster than most hunters can follow. It is tiring, so it is kept for emergencies.
- FR: D'un coup de queue, une crevette se projette en arrière plus vite que la plupart des chasseurs. C'est épuisant, alors elle le garde pour les urgences.

**3. Fear / La peur.** Unlocks: the first time fear rises and settles again. Sources: LSE2021, ELWOOD2009.
- EN: Crabs and hermit crabs learn to avoid a place where something hurt them, and will give up a good shelter to stay away from it.
- FR: Les crabes et les bernard-l'ermite apprennent à éviter un endroit où quelque chose leur a fait mal, quitte à abandonner un bon abri.

**4. Still wary / Encore inquiète.** Unlocks: leaving cover after a chase. Source: FOSSAT2014, to check.
- EN: After a bad moment, crayfish keep to dark corners for a while. Given a medicine that eases anxiety in people, they go exploring again.
- FR: Après un mauvais moment, les écrevisses restent un temps dans les coins sombres. Avec un médicament qui calme l'anxiété chez l'humain, elles repartent explorer.

**5. Grooming / La toilette.** Unlocks: the first time the shrimp grooms while idle. Source: BARR2008.
- EN: Shrimp clean their antennae often. In a 2008 study, prawns rubbed and groomed one antenna for a long time after an irritant touched it, and far less when given a local anaesthetic.
- FR: Les crevettes nettoient souvent leurs antennes. Dans une étude de 2008, des crevettes ont longuement frotté et nettoyé une antenne touchée par un produit irritant, et beaucoup moins sous anesthésique local.

**6. A new shell / Une nouvelle carapace.** Unlocks: first molt. Source: to check.
- EN: To grow, a shrimp leaves its shell behind. The new one is soft for a few hours, and the shrimp hides until it hardens.
- FR: Pour grandir, une crevette quitte sa carapace. La nouvelle reste molle quelques heures, et la crevette se cache le temps qu'elle durcisse.

**7. See-through / Presque transparente.** Unlocks: standing on three kinds of ground. Source: FAO.
- EN: A whiteleg shrimp is nearly transparent. Its tint shifts with the ground beneath it and the water around it.
- FR: La crevette à pattes blanches est presque transparente. Sa teinte change selon le fond et l'eau qui l'entoure.

**8. The journey / Le voyage.** Unlocks: reaching the open sea. Source: FAO.
- EN: Whiteleg shrimp are born in the open ocean. The larvae drift to the coast, grow up in lagoons and mangroves, and return to sea as adults at six or seven months.
- FR: Les crevettes à pattes blanches naissent en pleine mer. Les larves dérivent vers la côte, grandissent dans les lagunes et les mangroves, puis regagnent le large, adultes, à six ou sept mois.

**9. The next ones / Les suivantes.** Unlocks: spawning. Source: FAO.
- EN: A female releases between 100,000 and 250,000 eggs at a time. They hatch about sixteen hours later.
- FR: Une femelle libère entre 100 000 et 250 000 œufs à la fois. Ils éclosent environ seize heures plus tard.

**10. Able to feel / Capable de ressentir.** Unlocks: finishing chapter 2. Sources: LSE2021, UK2022.
- EN: In 2021, researchers in London reviewed more than 300 studies and found strong evidence that decapods, the group that includes shrimp, crabs and lobsters, can feel. The United Kingdom wrote this into law the next year.
- FR: En 2021, des chercheurs londoniens ont passé en revue plus de 300 études et trouvé des preuves solides que les décapodes, le groupe des crevettes, des crabes et des homards, peuvent ressentir. Le Royaume-Uni l'a inscrit dans sa loi l'année suivante.

**11. How many / Combien.** Unlocks: spawning. Source: RP2023. Optional: the author to keep or cut.
- EN: According to Rethink Priorities, shrimp are the animals most often killed for food: about 440 billion farmed shrimp each year.
- FR: Selon Rethink Priorities, les crevettes sont les animaux les plus tués pour l'alimentation : environ 440 milliards de crevettes d'élevage chaque année.

## Narration

Second person, present tense, from inside the body. French uses "tu". The player's shrimp is female, which fits both the French word and the spawning at the end.

Rules:
- Twelve words at most. One line at a time. At least 20 seconds between lines.
- Senses and body first. No facts, no numbers, no opinions.
- Never the word "shrimp" as a category. The narration does not know it is a shrimp.

## What goes in and what stays out

In the journal, in neutral wording:
- What it means to be able to feel.
- The 2008 antenna study.
- The 2021 review and the 2022 UK law.
- Learning to avoid harm.
- The number farmed each year, as one optional card.

Left out of the game:
- Farming and slaughter practices.
- Shops, brands, campaigns and organisations.
- Slogans, appeals and anything in the imperative.

Switzerland, Austria and Norway are also reported to recognise decapods in law. Not used until checked.
