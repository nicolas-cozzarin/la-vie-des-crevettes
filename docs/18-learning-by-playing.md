Written for decision D38: the player should learn as much as possible about shrimp biology, behaviour and suffering during the story, by playing, with science behind every item. Read with [07-science-and-message.md](07-science-and-message.md) for the wording rules, [10-feelings-and-states.md](10-feelings-and-states.md) for the rules these items plug into, and [12-goals-and-journal.md](12-goals-and-journal.md) for the journal.

## What was asked, and what does not move

The author, 2026-11-15: "Can you think about ways to add stuff to learn about shrimp biology and ethology and suffering as much as possible during the main game? Validated with science but fun to play."

What does not move:

- Play never preaches (D3). No text tells the player what to think or do.
- No animal is eaten. No death and no injury on screen. Unnamed shrimp of the shoal can go missing off-screen (D42).
- Calm to play, not a calm life (D41). Nothing gory.
- A fact is stated only in the journal, in the words allowed by doc 07, with a source.
- Nothing is in a release while its source is marked "to check".

So the question is how to teach a great deal without a single lesson. The answer used here: **a fact is first something the player does, then something the journal confirms.**

## Six ways a fact gets in

| # | Way | How it works | Example |
|---|-----|--------------|---------|
| 1 | The fact is the control | A real trait of the animal is how a key behaves. The player learns it with their hands | Walking finds food that swimming passes over, because a shrimp tastes with its legs |
| 2 | The body shows it | Something true and visible on the shrimp changes with what it lives | The heart, seen through the shell, misses a beat at a fright |
| 3 | The others show it | Another shrimp does the same thing, unprompted, where the player can see | A stranger rubs its antenna, freezes, sleeps, gives way |
| 4 | The journal confirms it | A card unlocks at the moment of doing. It says what was observed, by whom, in which animal | "Crayfish sleep." |
| 5 | The journal asks first | A card not yet unlocked shows as a pencil question. The answer is found by playing | "How does a shrimp know which way is down?" |
| 6 | The journal gathers it | Three new pages turn what was done into a body, a list of behaviours and, if the author wants it, a page on what people do | "Your body", "What shrimp do", "Among humans" |

Ways 1 to 3 are play and carry no words. Ways 4 to 6 are the journal, which the player opens when they choose. A small paper tag, the "small line" of doc 12, says when a card has arrived. Nothing else interrupts.

What makes each item fun is one of four things: a surprise (the larva travels tail first), a skill (ride the tide), a secret (stand still and the hidden life around you shows), or a reaction (the hermit crab's fear, a companion's sleep).

- Shallow, plant-rich water is poorest in oxygen at the end of the night, not at midday. "Thin water" below is therefore a dawn event. A reference for this daily cycle is still to find.

### One item apart: the sting

Decided by the author on 2026-11-16: built, with the journal naming the animal each step was seen in (D46). Rule 2 of `CLAUDE.md` now allows it: it hurts for a few seconds and leaves no wound.

The idea is to let the player live the one experiment the journal already quotes. In the 2008 study a prawn touched on one antenna by an irritant did not just flinch. It rubbed and groomed that antenna for a long time, and far less under a local anaesthetic (BARR2008). That gap between a flinch and what follows is what researchers mean when they say pain is more than a reflex.

| Step | What happens | Length |
|------|--------------|--------|
| Touch | The shrimp brushes the tentacles of a sea anemone on a rock. One tail flip, by itself | 0.25 s |
| Rub | It draws the touched antenna through its front legs, again and again. Walking is possible at 0.7 of normal speed and pauses the rubbing | 8 s |
| Sore | The antenna on that side is held low. Fear cannot fall below 0.2 | 20 s |
| Memory | A danger mark stays on that anemone for the rest of the visit. Within 2 BL of it the shrimp slows by a third when steered toward it. The player can push through | The visit |

There is no wound and nothing lasts. Other shrimp do the same when they touch one: a stranger flinches, rubs, and keeps away afterwards. One good food patch sits beside an anemone, so the player gets to weigh it up.

Where: one anemone in the mangrove's creek mouth and two in the lagoon's rock garden. The "Grooming" card unlocks here, and its title becomes "More than a reflex".

|------|--------|--------|----------|
| Touch: stung, jerks backward | CRAWFORD1992 | An anemone shrimp not yet used to its anemone | Read at second hand |
| Rub: long grooming of the touched antenna | BARR2008 | A prawn, with an irritant, not a sting | Reference confirmed. Read the paper before release |
| Memory: keeping away from where it hurt | MAGEE2013 | Shore crab | Read |

### What shrimp do

A list of behaviours drawn up by watching animals is called an ethogram, and it is how the study of behaviour begins. This page is the player's own. Twenty-nine entries.

| Group | Entries |
|-------|---------|
| Moving | Walk. Swim. Tail flip. Hold against a current. Ride a tide |
| Sensing | Flick the short antennae. Sweep the long antennae. Taste underfoot. Keep still and feel |
| Eating | Pick with the pincers. Favourite food |
| Looking after the body | Clean the antennae. Molt. Hide while soft. Burrow. Sleep |
| With danger | Freeze. Hide. Startle. Stay wary. Keep away from a place. Stop minding what is harmless |
| With others | Touch antennae. Follow. Graze side by side. Give way. Pass on an alarm. Follow at sea. Spawn |

The entries are the game's behaviours under their plain names, so the list costs no new rule. An achievement, "Field notes", is earned when every entry is inked.

### Level 4: the other life

**Why it is here.** By the end of the story the player knows what a shrimp needs because they have needed it: dark, the right sand, clean water, room, others they know, a way out. The pond life is the same animal with almost none of those. It is lived, not told. The keys are the same, and most of what they used to do finds nothing.

**What it is not.** No narration. No story steps, challenges or achievements. No names. Nothing to fail: the player cannot lose, and every key keeps working. No death, no wound and no body is ever shown (rule 2). No feed is shown being eaten (rule 1, T49).

#### The farm (T50)

The author chose a lined pond on 2026-11-16. An intensive pond, as the FAO sheet describes them: square, about 0.5 ha, 1.6 m deep, lined with black plastic over its floor and banks, stocked at 60 to 300 young shrimp per square metre. The game uses 100. Drawn at the pond life's scale, that is one shrimp about every 1.2 BL in every direction, as far as the player swims: about fifty on every screen, everywhere. "Heavy aeration" (FAO): paddle wheels churn at the edges and drive a slow current round the pond. Feed is given "4–5 times per day" (FAO), and is never shown (T49). Harvest is by draining through a bag net at the outlet gate, which FAO gives for intensive ponds too.

Where it stands: FAO says intensive farms are "commonly located in non-tidal areas", more and more far from the sea. This one stands behind the channel's bank, pumps its water from the channel and lets it out there through its gate. That keeps the pond seen in level 3 and the meeting at the mesh. Whether such a farm is usual on this coast is to check.

- While true: a stimulus worth less than 0.15 fear is ignored. The screen dims by 15% and sounds are softened.
- Any key ends it at once. A threat's `presence` ends it with a startle.
- A shrimp that has been `asleep` for 20 s or more is `rested` at the next dusk: comfort target +0.05 for that night. Nothing is taken from one that has not slept.

### `thinWater`

- Area data: `thinWater: [{ zone, phases }]`. In the lagoon: zone I, during the last quarter of the night and dawn.
- Inside, the condition `breathless` is true: the gill flutter of the rig doubles, the shrimp rides 0.3 higher, the Eat prompt does not show, the comfort target is 0.2 lower.
- Other shrimp leave the zone within 5 s. That is the hint.
- On leaving: comfort +0.15 at once, a warm note, the card.
- It never harms and has no timer. It stays in gentle mode, since it is a discomfort and not a chase.

### `ebbRide`

- A shrimp in `rest` or `walk` feels `CURRENT.bedShare` = 0.3 of a current. In any other mode it feels all of it.
