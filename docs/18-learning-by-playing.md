# 18. Learning by playing

Status: proposal 0.1, 2026-11-15. Written for decision D38: the player should learn as much as possible about shrimp biology, behaviour and suffering during the story, by playing, with science behind every item. Nothing here is built. Each item says whether it is ready, still to check, or waiting for a yes from the author.

Read with [07-science-and-message.md](07-science-and-message.md) for the wording rules, [10-feelings-and-states.md](10-feelings-and-states.md) for the rules these items plug into, and [12-goals-and-journal.md](12-goals-and-journal.md) for the journal.

## What was asked, and what does not move

The author, 2026-11-15: "Can you think about ways to add stuff to learn about shrimp biology and ethology and suffering as much as possible during the main game? Validated with science but fun to play."

What does not move:

- Play never preaches (D3). No text tells the player what to think or do.
- No animal is eaten. No death and no injury on screen.
- A fact is stated only in the journal, in the words allowed by doc 07, with a source.
- Nothing is in a release while its source is marked "to check".

So the question is how to teach a great deal without a single lesson. The answer used here: **a fact is first something the player does, then something the journal confirms.**

## Six ways a fact gets in

| # | Way | How it works | Example |
|---|-----|--------------|---------|
| 1 | The fact is the control | A real trait of the animal is how a key behaves. The player learns it with their hands | Walking finds food that swimming passes over, because a shrimp tastes with its legs |
| 2 | The body shows it | Something true and visible on the shrimp changes with what it lives | The heart, seen through the shell, misses a beat at a fright |
| 3 | The others show it | Another shrimp does the same thing, unprompted, where the player can see | A stranger rubs its antenna, freezes, sleeps, gives way |
| 4 | The journal confirms it | A card unlocks at the moment of doing. It says what was observed, by whom, in which animal | "Crayfish sleep." |
| 5 | The journal asks first | A card not yet unlocked shows as a pencil question. The answer is found by playing | "How does a shrimp know which way is down?" |
| 6 | The journal gathers it | Three new pages turn what was done into a body, a list of behaviours and, if the author wants it, a page on what people do | "Your body", "What shrimp do", "Among humans" |

Ways 1 to 3 are play and carry no words. Ways 4 to 6 are the journal, which the player opens when they choose. A small paper tag, the "small line" of doc 12, says when a card has arrived. Nothing else interrupts.

What makes each item fun is one of four things: a surprise (the larva travels tail first), a skill (ride the tide), a secret (stand still and the hidden life around you shows), or a reaction (the hermit crab's fear, a companion's sleep).

### One item apart: the sting

Needs a yes, because it touches rule 2 of `CLAUDE.md`. Default: not built.

The idea is to let the player live the one experiment the journal already quotes. In the 2008 study a prawn touched on one antenna by an irritant did not just flinch. It rubbed and groomed that antenna for a long time, and far less under a local anaesthetic (BARR2008). That gap between a flinch and what follows is what researchers mean when they say pain is more than a reflex.

| Step | What happens | Length |
|------|--------------|--------|
| Touch | The shrimp brushes the tentacles of a sea anemone on a rock. One tail flip, by itself | 0.25 s |
| Rub | It draws the touched antenna through its front legs, again and again. Walking is possible at 0.7 of normal speed and pauses the rubbing | 8 s |
| Sore | The antenna on that side is held low. Fear cannot fall below 0.2 | 20 s |
| Memory | A danger mark stays on that anemone for the rest of the visit. Within 2 BL of it the shrimp slows by a third when steered toward it. The player can push through | The visit |

There is no wound and nothing lasts. Other shrimp do the same when they touch one: a stranger flinches, rubs, and keeps away afterwards. One good food patch sits beside an anemone, so the player gets to weigh it up.

If the answer is yes: one anemone in the mangrove's creek mouth and two in the lagoon's rock garden. The "Grooming" card would unlock here, and its title would become "More than a reflex". That an anemone's sting does this to a shrimp is itself to check.

### What shrimp do

A list of behaviours drawn up by watching animals is called an ethogram, and it is how the study of behaviour begins. This page is the player's own. Twenty-nine entries.

| Group | Entries |
|-------|---------|
| Moving | Walk. Swim. Tail flip. Hold against a current. Ride a tide |
| Sensing | Flick the short antennae. Sweep the long antennae. Taste underfoot. Keep still and feel |
| Eating | Pick with the pincers. Favourite food |
| Looking after the body | Clean the antennae. Molt. Hide while soft. Burrow. Sleep |
| With danger | Freeze. Hide. Startle. Stay wary. Keep away from a place. Stop minding what is harmless |
| With others | Touch antennae. Follow. Graze side by side. Give way. Pass on an alarm. Follow at sea. Spawn |

The entries are the game's behaviours under their plain names, so the list costs no new rule. An achievement, "Field notes", is earned when every entry is inked.

## What people do to shrimp

Decision D39, open. The author works on this subject and asked for the game to teach about suffering as far as it can. Decisions D3, D4 and D35 and the "left out" list of doc 07 were written before that, and say: play never preaches, the net is one short chapter that explains nothing, nothing else man-made appears, and farming and slaughter stay out of the game. Three of those four would have to move for the game to say more. This section lays out four levels, each containing the one before. The first changes nothing.

| Level | Name | What the player meets | What it changes |
|-------|------|-----------------------|-----------------|
| 1 | Felt | The discomforts of a wild life, each relieved by what a shrimp does: thin water, glare, a far engine, wariness, the soft hours after a molt, and the sting if accepted. Journal cards on pain, stress, sleep and fear in crustaceans | Nothing. All of it is in "True things to play" |
| 2 | Told | Level 1, and a journal page, "Among humans": the numbers farmed and caught, what trawls bring up, mangrove turned into ponds, eyestalk removal, what a pond's water must hold. Neutral, sourced, unlocked by living the matching moment | Doc 07's "left out" list. T17 becomes one card among several |
| 3 | Seen | Level 2, and forty seconds in the channel: the shoal passes the wall of a pond. Through the mesh of its outlet are others like them, very many, who cannot follow. Nothing is said about it | D35: the pond wall is a third man-made thing. D4 is untouched |
| 4 | Lived | Level 3, and a second, separate life of three minutes, opened from the title screen after the story: a shrimp in a pond. It ends at the draining, with a cut to white before anything is shown | The tone. It would be the one place where calm does not return |

**Proposed: level 3.** It is the furthest the game can go while every pillar of doc 01 still holds. The player has spent fifteen minutes learning what a shrimp needs by needing it: dark, fine sand, clean water, room, a way out. The pond is the same animal with none of those, seen for a moment from the free side of a mesh. Nothing has to be explained, because the player already knows. Level 4 says the same thing with far more force and at a cost to the game's promise of calm. It is better judged after playtests of level 3, and it can be added later without changing anything else.

### Level 3: the pond, seen

Where: the channel, along the top edge of zone D, "the pool", where the tide slows and the shoal regroups. The stop is already there.

| Thing | Look from above | Does |
|-------|-----------------|------|
| `pond.wall` | A straight bank of packed earth along the edge, 2400 px long. The only straight line in the game | Solid |
| `pond.outlet` | A round pipe mouth in the wall, 3 BL across, closed by a mesh | Green-brown water comes through it into the channel |
| `pond.plume` | A stain of murkier, greener water spreading from the outlet, 10 BL long, bent by the current | Inside it Sense shows only the thin white ribbon of the unknown. The shrimp will not eat there |
| `pond.crowd` | Through the mesh: pale shapes of shrimp, close together, moving along the wall. No sand, no cover | One of them comes to the mesh when the player does |
| Sound | A slow, even beat of paddles on water, behind the wall | Fades in from 12 BL |

What happens, with the tide carrying the shoal past:

1. The water changes taste. The shoal slows. Narration, a "must" line: "The water tastes wrong here."
2. Nacre stops short. Brise goes to the mesh. If the player goes too, a shrimp on the other side comes up, and two antennae touch through the mesh. It counts as a greeting.
3. Narration: "Others, like you. So many."
4. The tide pulls. The shoal moves on. The one behind the mesh stays at it. Narration: "They do not follow."

Nobody is hurt and nothing is explained. The place gets a journal entry with a drawing and a name, "The wall", and no words, as "The shadow" has. With level 2 the card `card.inPonds` unlocks here.

To check before building: that ponds stand behind mangrove and lagoon shores on this coast and release water into channels, that their outlets are screened, and that paddle wheels run at night. The three lines above are new text and would go into doc 13.

### Level 4: the other life

Not proposed for now. Recorded so that the idea is not lost.

A separate entry on the title screen, shown after the story has been finished once. No story steps, no challenges, no narration. A bright tank, then a pond. The keys are the same and most of them find nothing: Hide finds nothing to go under, the floor is too hard to burrow, a tail flip ends at a wall. There is no hunter. The water runs thin before each dawn until the paddles start. After three minutes the level begins to fall, the current draws everyone one way, and the screen goes to white before the outlet. Then the title screen.

It would need its own decision on food (the game shows only algae being eaten, and a pond's feed is not that), and its own playtests.

## Rules for the items that add rules

Enough for `src/core/` and its tests. Numbers go in `src/data/tuning.ts` under the names given.

### `rideTide`, in Drift zone D

| Name | Value |
|------|-------|
| `TIDE.floodTime` | 12 s |
| `TIDE.ebbTime` | 8 s |
| `TIDE.floodSpeed` | 110 px per second, toward the coast |
| `TIDE.ebbSpeed` | 60 px per second, toward the sea |
| `TIDE.liftBonus` | 0.4: a larva that is moving during the flood is carried 40% faster |
| `TIDE.turnTime` | 1.5 s for the water to change direction |

- Holding Hide during the ebb drops the larva to the bed in 0.4 s. On the bed the water does not carry it.
- With no key at all the larva still gains 42 px per second on average, so nobody is stuck.
- Signs that the water has turned: the specks reverse, the water sound drops in pitch, the haze breathes outward.
- Events: `tide:turned { dir }`, `larva:held`.
- Tests: no input reaches zone E. Holding through each ebb and moving through each flood reaches it in under half the time.

### `footTaste`

- While the motion mode is `walk` and the shrimp's centre is inside a food patch: the patch brightens by 20% for 0.4 s, a soft note plays, and the Eat prompt shows.
- While the mode is `swim`, `dart` or `glide`: nothing, and no prompt until the shrimp stops.
- At most once per patch every 10 s. Event: `taste:underfoot { foodId }`.

### `stillSense`

- True when there has been no movement input for `STILL.after` = 1 s.
- While true, each creature within `STILL.range` = 8 BL that moves faster than 0.5 BL per second shows a ring at its place every 0.8 s. Rings show through the murk.
- The long antenna on the side of the nearest ring lifts by 20 degrees.
- During a threat's `cue` stage the antenna on the threat's side lifts 0.5 s before the first visible sign.

### `pulse`

| Mood or condition | Beats per second |
|-------------------|------------------|
| Asleep | 0.6 |
| Content | 1.0 |
| Calm | 1.2 |
| Hungry, lonely | 1.3 |
| Curious | 1.5 |
| Wary | 1.8 |
| Afraid | 2.4 |

- At each `startled`: the next beat comes 0.6 s late, then the rate is 1.6 times its value, easing back over 6 s.
- Drawn by the rig: a dark oval 0.05 BL long under the back of the head shell, at 35% strength, swelling by a quarter on each beat. Visible on another shrimp within 2 BL.
- `Pulse` lives in `src/core/feelings/`. Tests: the skipped beat, the rates, the easing.

### `habit`

- Kinds that can be got used to: `leafShadow`, `cloudShadow`, `turtleShadow`, `fishSchool`. Never `bird`, `fish`, `net` or anything the `ThreatDirector` starts.
- The fear gained from a kind is multiplied by 0.5 for each time it has been met. The count falls by one every 180 s.
- Test: the fourth leaf shadow in a minute adds less than 0.02 fear. A bird's cue after ten leaf shadows adds its full amount.

### `rightSand`

| Ground kind | Time to burrow | Result |
|-------------|----------------|--------|
| `fineSand` | 1.2 s | `burrowed`. Only the eyes and the tips of the short antennae show |
| `mud` | 1.8 s | `burrowed` |
| `coarseSand` | 2.5 s | Half buried: counts as `sheltered`, not as `hidden` |
| `rock`, `roots` | | Two scrapes, 0.6 s, then nothing |

- An area and each of its ground patches carry a `groundKind`.
- By day, resting on `fineSand` raises the comfort target by 0.1.
- The lagoon gets three patches of coarse sand in zones B and E, so that the difference is met in the story.

### `sleep`

- `asleep` becomes true after 5 s `burrowed` or `hidden`, by day, with no input and fear below 0.1.
- While true: a stimulus worth less than 0.15 fear is ignored. The screen dims by 15% and sounds are softened.
- Any key ends it at once. A threat's `presence` ends it with a startle.
- A shrimp that has been `asleep` for 20 s or more is `rested` at the next dusk: comfort target +0.05 for that night. Nothing is taken from one that has not slept.

### `thinWater`

- Area data: `thinWater: [{ zone, phases }]`. In the lagoon: zone I, during the last quarter of the night and dawn.
- Inside, the condition `breathless` is true: the gill flutter of the rig doubles, the shrimp rides 0.3 higher, the Eat prompt does not show, the comfort target is 0.2 lower.
- Other shrimp leave the zone within 5 s. That is the hint.
- On leaving: comfort +0.15 at once, a warm note, the card.
- It never harms and has no timer. It stays in gentle mode, since it is a discomfort and not a chase.

### `ebbRide`

- A shrimp in `rest` or `walk` feels `CURRENT.bedShare` = 0.3 of a current. In any other mode it feels all of it.
